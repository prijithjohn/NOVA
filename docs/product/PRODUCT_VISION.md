# NOVA Product Vision

## Vision

NOVA gives a person one trustworthy place to think clearly, plan deliberately, remember what matters, and act on it.

It is a Personal AI Operating System, not an autonomous replacement for the user. The system connects the user's Assistant, Memory, Goals, Tasks, Calendar, Finance, Documents, and Analytics into a coherent model of personal life and work.

## User Promise

When the user brings a question, commitment, idea, record, or decision to NOVA, NOVA should help them:

- understand the relevant context;
- turn intent into an achievable plan;
- retain information they choose to keep;
- identify and complete the next useful action.

## Product Character

NOVA should be calm, explicit, and dependable. It should expose where information came from, distinguish facts from suggestions, preserve user control, and make uncertainty visible. The product should become more useful through organized personal context, not through unsupported claims of universal automation.

## Operating Model

The Assistant coordinates reasoning across the domains. Memory preserves user-approved context. Goals define outcomes. Tasks and Calendar represent execution. Finance and Documents hold structured personal records. Analytics explains patterns and progress from those records.

The domains are connected, but their data ownership remains clear. AI can propose or prepare changes; validated backend services and normal business rules must govern mutations, with user confirmation where consequences require it.

## Boundaries

NOVA does not assume access to external accounts or services. Integrations, automation, and domain capabilities must be specified and validated before they are treated as product behavior. Privacy, authentication, authorization, and cross-user isolation are foundational requirements.

## Long-Term Direction

NOVA should mature from a well-defined personal workspace into a reliable operating layer for everyday decisions and follow-through. Each new capability should make the user's context more actionable while keeping the system inspectable, reversible, and grounded in data the user controls.
